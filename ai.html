Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app works without any registration or sign-in. Just install it and start learning.

Where is my progress stored?

All progress, streaks and certificates are stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no cloud account.

Does the app collect any data?

No. The app has no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, French and Spanish. The app follows your device language, and you can change it in the profile tab.

What do I learn in this app?

16 learning paths on artificial intelligence: from AI basics, prompting and judging AI output to deepfakes, AI rules like the EU AI Act, and choosing the right tools. Each path has beginner, advanced and expert levels.

How do I earn certificates?

Finish the lessons of a level and pass its final exam with 80% or more to receive a PaviLabs certificate, which you can export as a PDF.

Why do videos not play?

Lesson videos are hosted on YouTube and load only after you allow external content in the app, and they need an internet connection. Everything else works offline.

How can I delete my data?

Since all data lives on your device, deleting the app removes everything. There is no server-side copy.

I found a bug or wrong content. Where can I report it?

Email us at mastertheschooloflife@gmail.com. Please include your device model, app version and a short description. We usually reply within 48 hours.

About the app

Daily Dose of AI is a free AI literacy app by PaviLabs. It offers 16 learning paths with 240 lessons per language, daily portions sized to your learning goal, quizzes, daily practice tips for tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and Perplexity, and 48 certificates with final exams. There are no ads, no account and no data collection.
